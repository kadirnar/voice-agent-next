How it works

The microphone is captured with getUserMedia (echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain on) and sent to the agent as the Opus track of an RTCPeerConnection. The page fetches the ICE configuration from /config, gathers its candidates, and POSTs the SDP offer to /offer. The agent's voice comes back on the same connection and plays through an <audio> element, smoothed by the browser's adaptive jitter buffer. Transcripts, agent state and latency metrics arrive on a data channel. Once a second the page reports its playout delay (jitter buffer plus half the round trip), so the agent knows how much of its answer you actually heard when you interrupt it.
